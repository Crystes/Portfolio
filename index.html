Hydroponic Greenhouse Monitoring System

A soil-less hydroponic system built around an ESP32-S3 that monitors and automates the conditions plants need to grow. Environmental and nutrient-level sensors drive automatic climate and irrigation control, while a companion app gives full visibility and manual override from anywhere.

core controllerESP32-S3
climate sensingBME280 — temperature, humidity & pressure
ventilationDual fans, auto-switched on temperature threshold
lightingLDR-based light sensing with automated grow light
nutrient deliveryLiquid-fed (soil-less); ultrasonic level sensing with automated pump
remote controlLive monitoring & manual override via the Blynk app
local displayOnboard 16×2 LCD
Smart Energy Monitoring System
PIC16F876AThingSpeakPZEM-004T

Smart Energy Monitoring System

A microcontroller-based energy monitor built around a PIC16F876A that measures key electrical parameters and pushes them to the cloud for remote tracking. An ESP8266 module handles connectivity, controlled by the PIC over UART using AT commands.

core controllerPIC16F876A
measurementPZEM-004T — voltage, current, power, energy, frequency & power factor
connectivityESP8266, driven via UART using AT commands
cloud loggingReadings logged to ThingSpeak
local displayOnboard LCD
